FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PDF Ninja Pro really free?

Yes, PDF Ninja Pro is completely free to use. There are no hidden fees, no premium tiers, and no registration required. All features are available to everyone.

Is it safe to use PDF Ninja Pro?

Absolutely! PDF Ninja Pro processes all files directly in your browser. Your PDFs are never uploaded to any server. Your documents stay completely private and secure on your device.

What PDF operations can I perform?

You can split PDFs, merge multiple PDFs, convert PDF pages to images (JPG/PNG), extract text, rotate pages, reorder pages, add comments/annotations, add colored backgrounds, and convert images to PDF.

Do I need to install any software?

No installation required. PDF Ninja Pro runs entirely in your web browser. It works on Windows, Mac, Linux, and even mobile devices.

Are there any file size limits?

Since processing happens in your browser, the only limit is your device's memory. Most users can easily handle PDFs of 50-100MB or more.

Can I use PDF Ninja Pro on mobile?

Yes! The interface is fully responsive and works on smartphones and tablets. Access it from any modern mobile browser.
